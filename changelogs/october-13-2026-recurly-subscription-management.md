October 20th brings a clean mix of platform improvements across gateway onboarding, invoicing, and API parity. Nuvei is now visible and selectable in the Payment Gateways page for merchants worldwide, and CyberSource migrated merchants will see improved sandbox reliability with fixes to 3DS triggering and partial authorization handling. Invoice templates gain a new toggle to show or hide the merchant billing email address — on by default, so no action is needed unless you want to change the behavior.

For merchants managing external subscriptions, Google Play lifecycle events will now consistently populate `last_purchase`, and external subscription responses now include references to linked external products and plans. Account Hierarchy merchants gain new controls over when a child account’s billing target can change, and V3 API users will find improved parity between the `/subscriptions` and `/purchases` endpoints, with several invoice-level fields now supported on both.
