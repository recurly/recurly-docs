| Release Date | Feature                   | Type        | Potential Impact | Description / Overview                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------ | ------------------------- | ----------- | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sep 15       | Coupons                   | Enhancement | Low              | Previously, coupon codes that were not redeemed could only expire on a fixed calendar date or never expire, set once at bulk coupon creation, and applied uniformly to every unique code under that one bulk coupon. This release adds a third redemption mode — **Redeem By Window** — creating a _relative_ expiration period (e.g., 7 days, 2 weeks, 1 month) that starts individually for each unique code at the moment it's generated, rather than at coupon creation. |
| Sep 15-16    | **Cybersource Gateway**   | Enhancement | High             | Production migrations will take place for certain low to mid volume merchants using Cybersource. This will enable recent behind-the-scenes improvements for the gateway.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Sep 15       | **Chase Orbital Gateway** | Enhancement | Low              | Recurly now supports separate Authorization and Capture on the [Chase Orbital](https://docs.recurly.com/recurly-subscriptions/docs/chase-paymentech-orbital) platform. You can reach out to support or read our [documentation](https://docs.recurly.com/recurly-subscriptions/docs/auth-and-capture) for details on this behavior.                                                                                                                                          |
