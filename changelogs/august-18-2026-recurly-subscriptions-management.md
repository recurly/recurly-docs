This release brings analytics, gateway upgrades, and new retry behavior to Recurly.&#x20;

The **Checkout** dashboard will be updated to include a new payment method conversion section. This update with include a trend chart comparing overall conversion against each payment method  (credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, ACH, etc.) as well as a breakdown table for each method showing how often it's shown, attempted, and successfully charged — with two key rates: Checkout Rate and Authorization Rate.

**Stripe** will receive a maintenance update around API versioning as well as an enhancement for unscheduled merchant-initiated transactions using Auth and Capture logic.

**Retries** now maintain a dedicated list of Visa category 1 sub-codes for retries to enhance our already supported Mastercard MAC codes for those rare scenarios where Recurly receives them including: Stripe, when Stripe is your acquirer, and in Recurly Recover when they are provided via API.&#x20;

Finally, **Cybersource** is being upgraded behind-the-scenes with little to no change required from Merchants unless you have not enabled your configuration with a P12 REST certificate yet. Sandbox sites will be migrated first, followed by production sites between September and early October of this year.

Please reach out to support or your normal Recurly point of contact with questions on any of these release notes.
