# Definition

<div class="rp-definition">Dunning is the automated process Recurly uses to recover revenue from failed or declined transactions. This section brings together the reporting tools you need to monitor recovery performance, compare campaign strategies, and understand how dunning affects your subscription and revenue metrics.</div>

# Key details

## Dunning campaigns

Dunning management plays a central role in Recurly's revenue recovery efforts. These analytics tools help you monitor, analyze, compare, and enhance your dunning strategies to boost revenue recovery.
