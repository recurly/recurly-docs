## Overview

<br />Choose environment, enter client id, client secret and connect.

## Connecting

### <br />Choose Environment

You will be working with the Sandbox first to work through setup of E-Invoicing without submitting actual invoices requiring payment. &#x20;

Production Environment will be for your Recurly production site to connect to the Avalara ELR production site when ready to go live with chargeable invoices.

## Enter Credentials

<br />Enter client id and secret that you created when you setup your Avalara ELR instance.  <br />

## Connect

Finally, press Connect to have Recurly test your credentials to establish a connection with Avalara ELR. &#x20;

You will receive a success message upon successful connection or an error message if unsuccessful.&#x20;
