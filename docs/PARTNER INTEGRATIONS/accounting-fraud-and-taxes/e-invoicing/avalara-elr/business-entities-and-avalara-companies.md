## Overview

You have set up companies in Avalara and need to connect them with a Recurly Business Entity.  This mapping ensures documents are routed using the correct mandates and configurations.

## Configuration

The Avalara Company list is retrieved from the available active companies you had previously set up in Avalara.  The Business Entities are from your configured entities in Recurly.
