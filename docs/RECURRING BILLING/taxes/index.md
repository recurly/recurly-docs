# Definition

<div class="rp-definition">Taxes are mandatory charges applied to goods, services, and income — and in global ecommerce, staying compliant across jurisdictions is non-trivial. Recurly's tax tools calculate, apply, and manage taxes automatically, so your billing stays accurate and compliant whether you're selling locally or internationally.</div>

# Key details

<div class="rp-nav-grid">

<Cards>
  <Card title="Taxable address control" href="https://docs.recurly.com/docs/tax#determining-taxable-addresses" target="_blank">
    Control which addresses from each invoice are sent to your tax service for calculation, based on the tax settings configured on each of your business entities.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sales taxes" href="https://docs.recurly.com/docs/tax" target="_blank">
    Automatically calculate and apply the appropriate sales tax to transactions based on customer location and your tax configuration.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tax-inclusive pricing" href="#">
    Offer pricing that already includes tax, giving customers a clear, all-in price at checkout without surprises at payment.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tax location validation" href="https://docs.recurly.com/docs/tax-location-validation" target="_blank">
    Verify customer locations before applying tax to ensure the correct rates are used and reduce the risk of miscalculation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="European Union VAT" href="https://docs.recurly.com/docs/eu-vat-2015" target="_blank">
    Handle EU VAT requirements — including reverse charge rules, country-specific sequencing, and VAT number validation — for customers across EU member states.
